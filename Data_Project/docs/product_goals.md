Game Description
The system simulates a competitive multiplayer online game.
Players repeatedly join short matches, gain experience and resources, improve their abilities, and purchase equipment.
The core gameplay loop is:

Join session → Play matches → Progress → Return

The success of the game depends on long-term player retention and perceived fairness of matches.
Therefore, the data platform aims to understand player behavior, identify engagement patterns, and predict player churn.


Business Questions
Retention

    What percentage of players return one day after registration (D1 retention)?
    How many matches does a player typically play before leaving the game?
    Does inactivity between sessions predict churn?

Gameplay Quality
    Do unbalanced matches increase early exits?
    Do consecutive losses increase churn probability?
    Does matchmaking waiting time affect player retention?

Engagement

    Do players who purchase items stay longer?
    Does playing with friends improve retention?
    Does player progression speed affect engagement?

Prediction

    Can we predict whether a player will stop playing within the next 7 days?

Company Decision Making
The company will use the insights to:
    Send rewards to players predicted to churn
    Adjust matchmaking rules when matches are unfair
    Reduce queue waiting times
    Recommend playing with friends
    Suggest equipment upgrades to struggling players
