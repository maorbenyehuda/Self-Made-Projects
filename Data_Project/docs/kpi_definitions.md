🎯 Retention KPIs

DAU (Daily Active Users)
Number of unique players that logged in during a calendar day.

D1 Retention
Percentage of players who return to play one day after registration.

D7 Retention
Percentage of players who return within 7 days after registration.

Session Length
Average duration between session_start and session_end.

🎮 Gameplay Quality KPIs

Match Completion Rate
Percentage of matches that ended normally (not abandoned).

Early Exit Rate
Percentage of players leaving before match end.

Loss Streak Churn Rate
Probability of churn after 3 consecutive losses.

Match Balance Score
Average difference in score between teams.

💰 Engagement KPIs

Matches Per Player
Average matches played per active player per day.

Item Purchase Rate
Percentage of active players who bought at least one item.

Progression Speed
Average level increase per hour played.

🤖 Prediction Target

Churn Definition
A player is considered churned if no gameplay activity occurs for 7 consecutive days.
